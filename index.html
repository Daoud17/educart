About Educart

Educart, also known as the Center for Educational Consultation and Research, is a Lebanese-based institution specializing in teacher training, curriculum development, publishing, and educational consultancy. The center has built a strong presence in Lebanon and across the Arab world, supporting educational institutions with innovative solutions and expertise in the learning sector.

At Educart

Empowering Education Through Innovation, Knowledge, and Continuous Development.

Publishing

The publishing division specializes in developing academic books from kindergarten through higher education levels. Our materials are designed for both young and adult learners, with a strong focus on English as a foreign language. We emphasize meaningful learning through engaging activities, real-life situations, critical thinking, and purposeful reading and writing. Our services also include content development, editing, and the production of educational resources tailored to the needs of schools and institutions.

Curriculum Development

We provide curriculum support, development, and teacher training using modern teaching approaches. Our programs aim to enhance teaching methods and improve the overall learning experience in classrooms. Our approach combines research, practical application, and structured planning to build effective and adaptable curricula. We work closely with institutions to design programs that meet their specific educational goals and requirements.

Educational Consultation

Our consultation services include curriculum support, quality assurance, and teacher training. We design and deliver training programs, workshops, and seminars to support continuous improvement in teaching and school performance. We also assist institutions in implementing development plans, monitoring progress, and strengthening their educational systems. Our work includes developing materials, handbooks, and strategies that support long-term improvement.
